The probability connection

Compatible beliefs

01 · Accept gains

A free gain is desirable

Accept any gamble that never loses and wins in at least one outcome. This includes both positive axes, excluding the origin.

g ≥ 0, g ≠ 0 ⇒ g ∈ D
02 · Close under combination

Scale it. Add it.

If two gambles are desirable, every positive combination is desirable too. These implications generate the cone.

g, f ∈ D; α, β > 0 ⇒ αg + βf ∈ D
03 · Exclude zero

Keep acceptance coherent

Zero must not be desirable. Together with accepting gains and combining gambles, this also excludes every nonpositive gamble.

0 ∉ D

Finite assessment model: D = posi({g₁, g₂} ∪ {g ≥ 0 : g ≠ 0}). When coherent, this is the smallest coherent extension. Displayed values are rounded to two decimals. Probabilities shown in the bar form its closed dual; boundary probabilities can assign expectation zero to accepted gambles.
