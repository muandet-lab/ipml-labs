The lower-envelope representation

A cautious price, supported by every belief

P̲(f) = minp ∈ M Eₚ[f]

Buying f for μ gives the net gamble f − μ. For every μ below the lower prevision, this net gamble has strictly positive expectation under every probability in M. The lower prevision is the supremum of these buying prices.

P̅(f) = −P̲(−f) = maxp ∈ M Eₚ[f]

Selling f for μ gives μ − f. Prices above the upper prevision have strictly positive expected net gain under every probability in M. The upper prevision is the infimum of these selling prices.

At an endpoint, acceptance can depend on additional boundary commitments. Positive expectation under every model does not guarantee a positive realized payoff.

This app defines a coherent lower prevision on all two-outcome gambles using a nonempty closed interval of probabilities. Values are rounded to two decimals. Here the minimum is attained; for more general representations it is written as an infimum.
