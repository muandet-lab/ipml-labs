Assign evidence to sets

Exactly one outcome is true. Mass on {A, B} supports the claim that one of those two is true, leaving the choice between them open.

Edit either source. Each has a 100% budget. Any unassigned mass goes to Ω.

Explore an event

Belief sums mass on sets contained in the event. Plausibility sums mass on sets that intersect it. These give tight lower and upper probabilities over all ways of distributing each set’s mass among its members.

Combine evidence

Dempster’s rule models independent bodies of evidence on the same frame. Multiply each pair of masses and assign the product to the intersection. Products on the empty set form conflict K. Divide the remaining masses by 1 − K.
